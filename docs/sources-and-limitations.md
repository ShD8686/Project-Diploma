# Sources and what was not verified

**Web sources used:** CoolWalks (repository and Zenodo data); ASU "Cool Routes" (10 Jun 2026); HeiGIT HEAL and the technical post on heat-avoiding routing;
openrouteservice repository; UzDaily on Yandex Maps "Green" (13 Aug 2026); Android Authority on Google Maps "Prefer shade" (6 Nov 2025);
Techloy (secondary source, used only for the "60% crown" rule at Yandex and for the Cool Routes discrepancy).

**Papers:** Wolf, Viero, Szell (2025); Wang & Hou (CAADRIA 2025); Olaverri-Monreal et al. (2016); Deilami et al. (2020); Feng et al. (2024);
Vartholomaios (2023); Mansour et al. (2026); Al Shammas et al. (2023); Tomasi et al. (2024); Middel & Krayenhoff (2019); Taleghani et al. (2016).

**Not verified:** the Cool Routes and shaded.openrouteservice.org apps were not run; the CoolWalks code was not run; web pages were not re-opened when this documentation was prepared.
Minor inconsistencies in the primary texts (coordinates in Olaverri-Monreal's pseudocode, weight labels in Mansour) must be checked against the PDFs before citing.
