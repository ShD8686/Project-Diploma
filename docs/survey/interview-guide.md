# Interview guide (10-15 min, 3-5 people)

Before: say who you are, that it is for a diploma project, that it is anonymous and voluntary. Record notes only (no names).

1. When did you last walk 10+ minutes in the heat? Where, why? What was unpleasant?
2. Do you choose a route because of shade? How do you know where the shade is?
3. How much longer would you walk for a much shadier route? (Use 15 min: +2 / +4 / +6 minutes.)
4. Which apps do you use for walking routes? What is missing?
5. Show the mock-up (docs/diagrams/ui-mockup.png): is it clear? What would you change? Is the slider understandable?
6. Would you use it? When (time of day, purpose)?

After: fill the table in `docs/02-relevance-evidence.md` and mark which requirement each finding affects.
